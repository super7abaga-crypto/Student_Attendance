How to check in

  1. 1
    Ask your mentor to display the QR

    Session codes are short-lived, so scan the code that is on screen now.

  2. 2
    Allow camera and location

    Your browser will ask for both. The scan cannot be recorded without them.

  3. 3
    Scan and wait for confirmation

    Keep the QR code steady until the server confirms your attendance.

🔒 Your attendance is only recorded after the server verifies the session, the QR code and your campus location.
